# Before You Call: Quick Checks

Many service calls can be avoided with these checks. Agents should walk customers through these before booking a non-emergency visit.

## AC not cooling or blowing warm air
1. Confirm the thermostat is set to COOL and the set point is below room temperature.
2. Check the air filter. A clogged filter is the #1 cause of weak airflow. Replace if dirty.
3. Check the breaker panel. Reset any tripped breaker once. If it trips again, stop and book a visit.
4. Check the outdoor unit. If it is covered in debris or the fan is not spinning, book a visit.
5. If the indoor coil is iced over, turn the system OFF (fan ON) for 2 hours to thaw before the technician arrives.

## Furnace won't turn on
1. Confirm the thermostat is set to HEAT and the set point is above room temperature.
2. Check that the furnace switch (looks like a light switch near the unit) is ON.
3. Check the breaker.
4. Check the filter.
5. If you smell gas at any point, stop immediately and follow the safety protocol.

## Thermostat screen is blank
1. Replace the batteries if it has them.
2. Check the breaker for the air handler.
3. Check the float switch in the condensate pan — a full drain pan will cut power to the thermostat on many systems. If there is standing water, book a drain line clearing.

## Water pooling at the indoor unit
This is almost always a clogged condensate drain line. Turn the system off to prevent overflow damage and book a visit. Do not pour bleach into the line unless instructed by a technician.
