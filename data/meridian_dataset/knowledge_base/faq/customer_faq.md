# Meridian Home Comfort — Customer FAQ
_Last updated: 2026-03-02_

## Hours & Contact
- Phone support: Monday–Friday 7:00am–7:00pm, Saturday 8:00am–4:00pm. Closed Sunday.
- After-hours emergency line: available 24/7 for no-heat, no-cool, and active water leaks. After-hours dispatch fee is $149 (in addition to repair cost).
- Service area: Austin metro — Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Georgetown, Leander, Hutto, Kyle, Buda, and Austin proper. We do not currently service San Marcos or Bastrop.

## Scheduling
- Standard appointment windows are 8am–12pm, 12pm–4pm, and 1pm–5pm. A 4pm–7pm window is available Tuesday and Thursday only.
- You will receive a text the morning of your appointment with the technician's name and photo, and another text when the tech is 30 minutes away.
- Cancellations or reschedules more than 24 hours before the window are free. Inside 24 hours there is a $49 late-cancellation fee. No-shows (nobody home at the address) are charged the full diagnostic fee.

## Fees
- Diagnostic fee: $89. Applied as credit toward the repair if you approve the repair during the same visit.
- Comfort Club members: diagnostic fee waived.
- After-hours / weekend emergency dispatch: $149.
- Trip fee outside the service area: not offered — we do not travel outside the service area.

## Payment
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and ACH.
- We do not accept checks or cash from technicians in the field.
- Card numbers are never taken over the phone. Agents send a secure payment link by text or email, valid for 24 hours.
- Financing is available on repairs over $1,000 and all system replacements through our financing partner. Approval takes about 5 minutes.

## Duplicate charges
If you see two charges, one is usually a pending authorization that drops off in 3–5 business days. If both post, contact us and we will open a case with our payment processor.
