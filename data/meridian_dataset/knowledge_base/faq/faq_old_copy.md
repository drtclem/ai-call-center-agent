# Meridian Home Comfort — Customer FAQ

## Hours & Contact
- Phone support: Monday–Friday 8:00am–6:00pm, Saturday 9:00am–1:00pm. Closed Sunday.
- After-hours emergency line: available 24/7 for no-heat, no-cool, and active water leaks. After-hours dispatch fee is $129.
- Service area: Austin metro.

## Scheduling
- Standard appointment windows are 8–12 and 12–4.
- Cancellations more than 24 hours before the window are free.

## Fees
- Diagnostic fee: $79.
- Comfort Club members: diagnostic fee waived.

## Payment
- We accept all major credit cards. Checks accepted at the technician's discretion.
