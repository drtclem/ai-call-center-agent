# Comfort Club Maintenance Plan
_Effective: 2026-01-01_

The Comfort Club is Meridian's annual maintenance membership for residential HVAC systems.

## Plans

### Comfort Club (Standard)
- Price: **$189 per year** per system, or **$16.99 per month**
- Each additional system on the same address: **$99 per year**
- Includes:
  - Two seasonal tune-ups per year (one cooling, one heating)
  - Priority scheduling — members are booked ahead of non-members
  - Diagnostic fee ($89) waived on all service calls
  - 15% off all repairs (parts and labor)
  - No after-hours dispatch fee on emergency calls
  - Annual reminder and filter-change notifications

### Comfort Club Plus
- Price: **$269 per year** per system
- Everything in Standard, plus:
  - Annual water heater flush and inspection
  - Annual whole-home plumbing inspection
  - 15% off plumbing repairs
  - $500 off any full system replacement (Standard members also receive this)

## Billing and renewal
- Annual plans bill once on the enrollment date and auto-renew each year on the same date.
- Monthly plans bill on the enrollment day of each month and require a 12-month minimum commitment.
- Members receive a renewal reminder email 30 days before renewal.
- Existing members are locked at their enrollment price until their next renewal date, at which point the current price applies.

## Cancellation
- Members may cancel auto-renewal at any time before the renewal date.
- Annual plans are non-refundable once a tune-up has been performed in the current term.
- If no tune-up has been performed, a prorated refund is available within the first 60 days.
- Monthly plans cancelled before the 12-month minimum are charged the remaining balance of the minimum term.

## Upgrades
- Standard members may upgrade to Plus at any time. The difference ($80) is prorated for the months remaining in the current term.

## Transfer
- Memberships are tied to the service address and transfer to a new homeowner at the same address. They do not transfer to a new address.
