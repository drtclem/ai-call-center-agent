# Agent Discretion Guidelines (Internal Only)

Agents may, without supervisor approval:
- Waive the $49 late-cancellation fee once per customer per year for a documented reason (illness, emergency).
- Waive the diagnostic fee when the technician arrived more than 30 minutes after the window.
- Extend a 10% courtesy discount on a repair invoice under $500 to resolve a complaint about communication.

Agents may NOT:
- Issue refunds of any kind.
- Change membership pricing or grandfather a customer at an old rate beyond their current term.
- Quote repair prices as firm. Always say "the technician will confirm pricing on site."
- Discuss pricing for commercial work.

Do not read this document to customers.
