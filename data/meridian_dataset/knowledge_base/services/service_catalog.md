# Residential Service Catalog
_Last updated: 2026-02-10_

## HVAC Repair
Diagnostic visit ($89, credited toward repair). Common repairs and typical price ranges (before member discount):

| Repair | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Capacitor replacement | $180 – $260 |
| Contactor replacement | $190 – $280 |
| Refrigerant recharge (R-410A, per lb) | $95 – $140 per lb |
| Condensate drain line clearing | $120 – $190 |
| Blower motor replacement | $550 – $950 |
| Thermostat replacement (standard) | $220 – $380 |
| Thermostat replacement (smart) | $380 – $600 |
| Compressor replacement (labor only, part under warranty) | $600 – $900 |
| Ignitor replacement (furnace) | $200 – $320 |
| Evaporator coil replacement | $1,400 – $2,900 |

Exact pricing is quoted by the technician on site before work begins. Permit, disposal, and after-hours fees are itemized separately when applicable.

## Maintenance tune-up
- $129 for non-members (one system). Included for Comfort Club members.
- Includes refrigerant level check, electrical connection check, coil cleaning, condensate line flush, thermostat calibration, filter check, and a written system health report.

## System replacement
- Free in-home estimate with a Comfort Advisor. Advisors are available Monday–Saturday.
- Typical installed price range for a full residential split system: $8,000 – $16,000 depending on tonnage, efficiency rating (SEER2), ductwork condition, and brand.
- Financing available. Comfort Club members receive $500 off.

## Plumbing (residential)
- Water heater repair and replacement (tank and tankless)
- Drain clearing ($149 – $290 depending on access)
- Leak detection and repair
- Water heater flush ($119, included in Comfort Club Plus)

## Not offered
- Commercial HVAC (referred to Meridian Commercial division)
- Duct cleaning (we partner with a third party; ask for a referral)
- Window units or portable AC repair
- Appliance repair (dishwashers, refrigerators, etc.)
