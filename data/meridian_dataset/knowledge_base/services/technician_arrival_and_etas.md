# Technician Arrival Windows and Delays

Appointment windows are 4-hour blocks. Technicians arrive at any point within the window.

If a technician is running late:
1. Dispatch will text the customer with an updated ETA.
2. If the technician will miss the window entirely, the agent should offer to reschedule at no charge or keep the appointment with the diagnostic fee waived.
3. Agents may waive the diagnostic fee for delays over 30 minutes past the window without supervisor approval.

Customers are not required to wait past the window. A missed window is never charged as a no-show.
