# Agent Escalation & Safety Protocol
_Internal — Call Center — Rev 2026-01_

## Immediate safety escalation (do NOT troubleshoot)
If the caller reports any of the following, instruct them to **leave the home immediately and call 911 or their gas utility from outside**, then flag the account as priority emergency:
- Smell of gas / rotten eggs
- Carbon monoxide detector alarming
- Visible sparks, smoke, or flames from any unit
- Electrical burning smell combined with tripped breakers

Do not advise the customer to flip switches, open windows near the furnace, or restart equipment.

## Supervisor escalation (callback within 1 business day)
- Billing disputes where the customer disagrees with an invoice amount
- Refund requests of any amount
- Complaints about technician conduct or workmanship
- Requests to speak with a manager
- Any threat of legal action or chargeback

Log the case with the customer's preferred callback window. Do not promise a specific outcome.

## Department redirects
- Commercial projects → Meridian Commercial (estimator callback within 1 business day)
- Employment inquiries → careers page on website; do not take applications by phone
- Media or vendor solicitations → marketing@ inbox
- New system sales → book a Comfort Advisor estimate (not a service tech)

## What agents can resolve directly
- Scheduling, rescheduling, cancelling, and confirming appointments
- Explaining invoice line items
- Sending payment links
- Comfort Club enrollment, upgrades, renewal questions, and cancelling auto-renew
- Warranty coverage questions and booking warranty diagnostics
- Service area and hours questions
