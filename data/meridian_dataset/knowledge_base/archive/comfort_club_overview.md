# Comfort Club Maintenance Plan

The Comfort Club is Meridian's annual maintenance membership for residential HVAC systems.

## Plans

### Comfort Club (Standard)
- Price: **$149 per year** per system, or **$12.99 per month**
- Each additional system: **$79 per year**
- Includes two seasonal tune-ups, priority scheduling, diagnostic fee waived, and 10% off repairs.

### Comfort Club Plus
- Price: **$219 per year**
- Adds annual water heater flush and plumbing inspection.

## Cancellation
Members may cancel at any time for a full prorated refund of unused months.

## Transfer
Memberships transfer with the homeowner to a new address within the service area.
