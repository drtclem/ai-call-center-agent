# Discovery: final bucket list (signed off by owner, 2026-10-19)

_Source: `buckets_draft.md` (Opus proposal from 420 pass-1 lines, cross-checked by embedding clusters). Owner changes: burning smell moved from repair to safety emergency (D11). T3 labelers also get an `other` option (not a bucket) to catch leftovers._

| id | name | definition | includes | excludes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `repair_service_booking` | Book repair / diagnostic visit | Customer reports a non-emergency equipment or plumbing problem and wants a technician scheduled. | AC blowing warm or not cooling, furnace won't turn on, blank thermostat, grinding noise, water pooling at indoor unit, backed-up kitchen sink, water heater pilot going out. Also covers waitlist requests and questions about whether the maintenance plan would lower the cost of this repair. | Gas, CO or sparks emergencies. Warranty-coverage questions on failed equipment. Annual tune-ups. Any burning smell (safety emergency). |
| `maintenance_tuneup_booking` | Book annual tune-up | Customer wants to schedule routine annual HVAC maintenance. | Annual tune-up scheduling, including waitlist requests. | Visits for a reported problem. |
| `existing_appointment_mgmt` | Manage existing appointment | Customer is calling about an appointment that is already booked. | Reschedules, cancellations because the issue was already fixed, confirming an appointment window, and technician no-shows (window passed with no arrival or call). | New bookings. |
| `billing_payments` | Billing & payments | Routine account balance, payment, or charge-explanation requests that are not disputes. | Balance lookups, paying an invoice (via a texted secure link), explaining an unrecognized invoice and emailing an itemized copy, and suspected double charges. | Disputes over quoted vs. invoiced price. Refund demands. Membership autopay. |
| `membership` | Membership (Comfort Club) | Questions or account changes about the maintenance membership. | Plan info and price (including the $149 check), renewal charge timing, cancelling membership, autopay setup, upgrading to Plus, and member discount on a new system. | Questions about whether the plan would save money on a repair being booked; those go to the repair bucket. |
| `warranty` | Warranty questions & claims | Questions about warranty coverage, registration, transfer, or filing a claim. | Warranty claims on last year's install, compressor coverage (often followed by booking a repair), labor warranty on a repeat blower failure, manufacturer registration status, and warranty transfer to a new homeowner. | Generic repairs with no warranty question. |
| `replacement_estimate` | Residential replacement estimate | Customer wants a ballpark cost for replacing an aging home system and/or a free in-home estimate. | Replacement of an 18-year-old system, ballpark price, and booking a comfort advisor visit. | Commercial quotes. Member discount questions. |
| `complaints_disputes` | Complaints, disputes & refunds | Customer is dissatisfied and needs human or manager resolution. | Invoice higher than the quote (after-hours, disposal or permit fees), refund for a failed repair, disputed $4,000 replacement recommendation, rude technician, and repeated unreturned calls. | Neutral invoice questions or double-charge checks. |
| `safety_emergency` | Safety emergency | Potentially hazardous situation needing immediate safety guidance and dispatch. | Gas smell, carbon monoxide alarm, sparks from the outdoor unit. Any burning smell (from vents or equipment). | Non-hazardous equipment problems. |
| `non_customer_other` | Non-service / out of scope | Calls not about residential service for an existing or prospective customer. | Job/hiring inquiries, wrong number (Meridian Dental), and commercial rooftop replacement quotes. | Any residential service need. |

Labeler-only option: `other` — the call fits none of the buckets above. Expected count: 0; any hits are reviewed.
